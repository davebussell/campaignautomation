Read-only by default

We start where we can look but not touch. Write access is per workflow, scoped to the job, and revocable by you at any time.

Your data never trains models

Your campaigns, CRM and strategy docs run your workflows and nothing else. Never pooled across clients, never used for training.

Every action is logged, and reversible
