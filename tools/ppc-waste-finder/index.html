PPC Waste Finder.

Paste a search-terms report from Google Ads and get your wasted spend, your worst offenders, and a copy-ready negative-keyword list — in seconds, in your browser. Your data never leaves this page: there is no upload, no server, no account.

100% in-browserNo signupCopy-ready negatives

1 · Paste your search-terms report

In Google Ads: Campaigns → Insights & reports → Search terms → Download → CSV. Open it and paste everything here (or paste straight from the spreadsheet — tabs work too).

✓ Private by design — the analysis runs entirely in this tab. Close the page and it's gone.

What it finds

Terms that spent money and never converted
✓
Recurring words inside those terms (n-grams)
✓
A negative-keyword list, formatted and copy-ready
✓
Waste as a share of everything you spent
✓

Column names are detected automatically — it looks for search term / query, cost / spend, clicks, and conversions in any order, and skips Google's header preamble rows.

Results
–Spend with zero conversions
–Share of total spend wasted
–Zero-conversion terms
–Clicks that led nowhere

Worst offenders

Negative-keyword candidates

Recurring words across your zero-conversion terms, ranked by the money behind them. Review before adding — a word that looks wasteful in one campaign can be a winner in another.
